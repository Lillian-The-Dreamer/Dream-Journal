Swamp parkour

A good dream

I was watching a TV show that Henry and sully recommended to me. It was a Korean show with 10 seasons about an American guy living in Japan, but engaged to a woman in korea (arranged marriage, they've never met)

One night, the guy is walking home, and he meets a woman. They decide to get into parkour together, but specifically parkour on this river nearby. The next day, the man and the woman and one of the man's friends all go to this river. It is very green and swamp like, and there are signs warning about various dangerous creatures in it, such as crocodiles, gila monsters, octopi, etc.

At this point I've kind of become the guy's friend, rather than watching the show from the outside.

We start running towards the water, which has some trees and sticks and other stuff that we can run on without falling in, but we all fall in pretty immediately and have to swim to the next shore (the river is really more like a series of long pools, as each pool isn't connected and there's land bars between them.

We get to the next land bar and prepare to not fall in on this one. It has a large tree branch type thing on the left hand side, which I start running/swinging on. It is shaped sort of like a wiggly line, going up and down. Me and the woman are running along, and she reaches a point where she does a cool trick called "Satan's head" where she jumps off the tree right before it reaches the satanic point (part of this tree was Satan, or at least satanic in some way)

I keep running along the Satan part without falling into the water like she did, and I see a purple octopus with blue spots. I think it's probably deadly so I yell to warn the woman who is in the water. Then I reach a part why I swing a really long way on the tree, which is moving by itself now. I swing super far and reach the far shore, which is back in civilization away from the swampy area. The woman is already there, and very sad because she didn't go the whole way without falling into the water. I said that it would be fine because we were gonna do this every day and eventually she would get really good.

End of dream
