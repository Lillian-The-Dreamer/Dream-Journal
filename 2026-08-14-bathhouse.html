Bathhouse

A bad dream

I was at a large bathhouse. It was pleasant at first, but a cataclysmic storm began outside, which made all the lights go super dim and the water turned black, which was unpleasant.

After a little bit, the storm dies down, and I go outside. It's perfectly sunny and dry somehow, and I see a poison dart frog on the ground. It is purple with black spots. I decide that it must not be poisonous, because they don't live around here so it wouldn't have the right diet to make the poison, but I still don't pick it up anyway. I take a bunch of pictures, and go back inside to show Phoenix.

The building is now more like a bookstore. I wander around for a while trying to find Phoenix, and I eventually spot him in the magic the gathering section of the store.

There are tables here with people at them, and I realize that it's a cedh tournament and I signed up for it, and I'm almost late to my match. I sit down and start shuffling my deck. I'm playing Rog/reyhan, but when my opponent asks me about my deck, I start talking about cards that definitely wouldn't be in the deck (like phyrexian altar). I keep mulliganing and my hands are so so bad. I comment on it, and one of my opponents is like "oh yeah lol that's because I took the good cards out of your deck," and they put some cards back into my deck. I start shuffling again, but I see some Pokemon cards have been mixed into my deck now by the same person.

I try to take them all out, but there are so many that it's taking a long time. One of my opponents, a trans woman, says "dude what's taking you so long we're gonna call a judge on you" and I'm like "this person literally keeps fucking with my deck what am I supposed to do??" And the tgirl says "no I don't think anyone is messing with your deck I think you're just the worst at this game."

I look down at my deck and I realize that now it's a totally different deck, differentiated by the fact that the sleeves are pink now instead of green. I look in my deck box and see that rog/reyhan is still in there. The tgirl makes another mean comment and I throw my deck at her. She says "dude you should just resign at this point." I walk over and my deck is like totally under a big vent, and so I lean down to pick it all up, and say "fine, I resign"

After I pick it up and get back to my seat, some other guy is there defending me and arguing with the tgirl, but I am still so upset.

End of dream
