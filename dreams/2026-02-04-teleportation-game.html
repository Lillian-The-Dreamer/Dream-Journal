Teleportation Game

A good dream

Henry and Florian and I were playing a game where we were controlling a mech that was basically a house with legs and guns. It was pvp, and each mech was a team of 3 people doing movement, guns, etc. we had a large house so we killed a lot of people by stepping on them, and we also had missiles. We had a good strategy of disguising ourselves as a real house and then surprising people. After we lost, we had to go meet up in a field with everyone else who was playing the game. We got given devices that would teleport us around, and we had some objective that I couldn't remember. Our team was going to teleport somewhere in Vermont but I accidentally misclicked and went to some big mountain in New York. Only the first teleport was pickable, so after that I had to teleport randomly, and I was only allowed to teleport after walking 5 miles. I walked down a bit of the mountain and went into a McDonald's to use the bathroom, but inside it was actually this huge mall type thing with food and stores and such. There was a really cool thrift store that was also a cafe, and it had huge slices of cake (like 1.5 feet tall) that looked very good. They were strawberry with cream and also a chocolate section and a vanilla section. The sign said they were $5 but I decided not to get one for some reason, but I took a picture to send to Phoenix. I go through a door and it leads to a semi outside area that gives ski lodge vibes. I don't like that area so I go back out of the building. Trevor Snapchats me his coordinates but every time I go into Google maps I forget what they are, and then I go look at them again and then I forget again so I never end up figuring out where he is.

End of dream
