Demon hunting

A bad dream

I was in front of my dorm, sitting on the concrete steps. My mom was also there, and we were discussing "words with friends winter edition," and speculating about how it might be different from normal words with friends. After a while I left to go meet up with Lindy.

Later, me, Lindy, and Diana were in a house that wasn't exactly like either of our houses, but might have been either one. We were inspecting and modifying an art display that was made out of yarn hanging in various patterns. It was very high up in the room, and the other two were on ladders, but I was hanging precariously off the side of one of their ladders while holding the yarn to keep myself steady. Even though it was only like an 8 foot drop or so, I knew that if I fell I would die.

After a while of discussing the yarn, I decided to go down to the floor. I sat down, but Diana said that I shouldn't sit next to the window, since I was too important to die and she had the shotgun and could tank some hits from the thing we were fighting. At this point, it became clear that we were fighting/hunting/being hunted by a large demon creature, that currently took the form of a really big dog. It was weak to fire and silver, so we were making a lot of molotovs, even though we were fighting it in what was now clearly our house. I was trying to make molotovs, but I couldn't find any cloth and there was a constant threat of the demon attacking. At some point Alex arrived to help out, and at this point we knew that the demon was in Dad's room.

I was still looking for cloth and a lighter when it attacked. My only weapon was a small bottle full of alcohol. The bottle was weird and came to a sort of squeeze bottle tip rather than a normal hole at the top. The demon was attacking and biting me and trying to grab me, but I was doing a pretty good job of dodging while stabbing it with the alcohol bottle, which was doing nothing.

I could hear the demon's inner monologue, in which it revealed that it was very scared of my weapon because it could maybe make fire. I also learned that it was scared of fire because it killed without causing wounds (I guess kind of true??) because it got way stronger when wounds broke the skin, so fire would kill it without damaging it and making it stronger.

I heard all of this information, but it grabbed me and was tearing me up really bad, so I grabbed a knife off of a table and cut some huge holes into the side of the demon. It got way stronger and lots of demon fog stuff leaked out of the wounds, but it did back off for a little bit.

I was bleeding really bad from my chest and stomach, so I ran to the counter and grabbed an avocado, which I mashed up and rubbed on my wounds and then commanded it to "heal me," which actually worked and the avocado started sealing up my injuries, at least temporarily. I ran upstairs to my mom's room and found that she was watching YouTube, which annoyed me a little because I was fighting for my life. I told her that she had to heal me because I was gonna die, even with the avocado.

End of dream
