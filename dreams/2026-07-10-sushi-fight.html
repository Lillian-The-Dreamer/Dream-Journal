Sushi fight

A good dream

I was homeless or maybe just very poor. There was this guy who I had an arrangement with that we would fight every so often, and if I won he would give me like $1000 and if he won he would get half of all my money.

We fought by eating sushi. He would buy a large amount and we would each eat one roll at a time. Whoever did it better would win that round. It was going okay, but I ended up with a roll that had blackberries and blackberry sauce, and some of it was just pieces of broccoli. The guy was very sure he was gonna win, but I pulled out a crazy move. I dipped my broccoli into his sauce that was spicy and salty and red. I looked him and said "you thought that I was done for because broccoli needs something salty to soak into it but I only had blackberry sauce." And then I show him that I dipped it into his sauce to use the flavor. The entire audience goes crazy. He is so mad that he shows me a vision of him blowing up my house, and then forfeits and says that I can have his credit card.

End of dream
