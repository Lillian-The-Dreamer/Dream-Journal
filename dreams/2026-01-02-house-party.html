House Party

A good dream

In this dream, I was at a weird larger version of Chuck and Mary's house, and there was a new years party going on.

There was this kid there who wasn't invited and no one knew him, and I decided that if anyone found him that they would probably kill him and also me for associating with him, so I had to sneak him out of the house.

After a while of hiding him in various rooms, my mom came in from running outside and I asked if she just did the beer mile (1 mile run but you have to drink a beer every quarter mile), and she said no. Outside there was a line of shirtless men who were running and drinking beer so I thought that she might have been a part of that, but she wasn't. I then went back to getting the kid to escape and eventually got him outside and onto the street, but I had to lie to some housekeepers that were chasing us that he was actually invited to the party.

After that, I went back inside and got on a PC in the living room and started playing this game that was sort of like risk of rain 2 mixed with nightreign. It had ror2 items and skill mechanics but it had nightreign like bosses and leveling and the time limit with the circle. There was also a mechanic where you could find and collect various plants by playing this sort of rhythm minigame where you had to drag an orb down a line in time with the notes.

I was playing a character that could either start with a close range ice attack that was basically icicle from yomi hustle, it had both the grounded and airial modes. The other option was a weird randomly pointed electricity beam attack that bounced across the screen, and a supporting skill that recharged it.

I chose the icicle version, and I was playing but got stuck at a point where I couldn't find the next place to go in the map, because it was sealed with a barrier. I googled it and it told me there was an illusory wall in a cliff face that had an NPC character that could unseal the barrier. I found the NPC but it wouldn't unseal the barrier, so I went into the menu and did some crazy shit (reminiscent of caves of qud shit) where I turned the NPC into a png item but kept all of their characteristics, which ended up being approximately 750 megabytes, and I picked up the item and then dropped it through the barrier and then turned it back into the NPC who hit a lever on the other side to unseal the barrier. The boss down here was dogs and they were very difficult and I died.

Later on, I was playing again with Lindy and Hazel. I was still playing the icicle character. We were doing pretty good, I got a yellow item (best tier, as per nightreign) called women's hardships and it said that it made me more powerful when men were sad. Later I looked it up and found out that what it actually did was buff my electricity attacks by 50% and whenever they hit, there was a chance to recharge all my electricity abilities. I thought "damn, I should've played the electricity version of this character," and told Lindy and Hazel that if they found any electricity attacks they should give them to me. I also found a plant to collect but I messed up the minigame and only collected a low tier version of it. I also found some styrofoam food containers that each had 1 food item in them, labelled by how much money that food item was worth. They were mostly various sushi rolls but they were all bad kinds.

Finally we went to the dog boss and they were really hard and killed us all again.

End of dream
