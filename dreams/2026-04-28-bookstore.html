Bookstore

A good dream

Trevor and I were walking down main street, headed for church street. Our goal was to go to a bookstore to find a new book for me to read. We walked into the store, and there was an old man and his wife inside. I told him that I was looking for a book that was kind of like Suzanna Clarke or Brandon Sanderson, but less of a big 1200 page book. He pulled out a bunch of suggestions and brought me over to a table in a corner to look at them.

One of them was called "mistlake" and the description on the back said

"Don't jump into a mistlake unless you want demons in your house. Or at least, that's what the populous thinks. One day, a man tries to kill himself by jumping into the lake, but is surprised when he falls through the mist into a hallway. Even more surprised are the "trogues," little creatures who live in burrows surrounded by the mistlake. Now they must team up, because there's a dragon coming!" (There was a little more exposition in the description but this is all that I remember)

It was very thin and seemed like a children's book, so I rejected it. The man went to get more books, but while he was at the front, I heard him telling his wife that my jacket was extremely dangerous and powerful, and she asked how powerful, and he said that it was powerful enough to make their whole security system worthless.

I had put my jacket over my chair, and was not wearing it at this point.

The man came back with books and some old antique letters and notebooks and stuff. I looked over them but didn't want any, so I thanked him "very very very much." He was shocked at how many verys there were, and I said "well, at least very very. The third one is up for debate" and he said that that was more reasonable.

He walked off, and I started walking away too. I was halfway across the store when I realized I left my jacket on my chair. I started sprinting back for it, but the old couple and someone who was seemingly their bodyguard also started running for it.

I knew that I had to put the jacket on to be powerful so they wouldn't kill or hurt me, but I didn't have time to put it on before they were gonna get to me, since the bodyguard had a long mancatcher (like those sticks with grabbers on the end).

I threw the jacket across the table towards him and put my hands in the air and started yelling "MISUNDERSTANDING MISUNDERSTANDING MISUNDERSTANDING MISUNDERSTANDING" to signify that I wasn't planning on using the jacket on them.

It worked, and the mancatcher guy didn't hurt me.

End of Dream
