Duel

A bad dream

I can't remember the whole thing but I had to duel my mom/Dad (they were kind of the same person). There were all kinds of rules but mostly there was a ceremony thing at the start and then my mom came into the room. I had prepared my dueling pistol by taking out the bullet cartridge and filling it with about $337 of gold pieces, making it very heavy. I also kept my knife in my back belt loop.

I was considering surrendering or trying to talk it out, but my mom came into the room already holding her gun out (it was like a nerf Jolt but shot an actual bullet. I pulled my gun as she shot me and hit me in the leg. While she was trying to reload, I ran up close and shot her in the stomach, hoping that the improvised slug would go through to the spine. She fell down and was like "damn" and I took my knife and stabbed her in the neck. At this point I realized that I actually didn't want to kill her because there was no reason to and she's my mom. I go grab a bandaid but I feel like the odds aren't good since I shot a slug through her and stabbed her in the neck. By the time I get back though , like 5 second later, she's downstairs talking with my dad. I go downstairs and she's totally fine, she said that the bullet hit her in a safe place and the knife missed the important parts of her neck.

She revealed to me that she and my dad had been dueling for a hundred years and the score between them was perfectly tied.

End of dream
