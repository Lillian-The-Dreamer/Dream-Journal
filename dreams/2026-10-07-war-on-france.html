War on France

A bad dream

I dreamt I was in the British army, and we were at war with France. I had a cloak but it was blue, and I needed to get a green one before I was deployed.

I didn't have the money for this, but I was told by an officer that I could steal stuff from the warehouse, because the clothes were made with French cloth.

Me and some of my companions went down to the warehouse, which was achieved by going down of those things that's all the colorful metal poles that have balls on them (the things you might find for kids to play with in a waiting room). This one was really big though and human sized.

We are stopped by some officers who want to search us. They say it's in case we have contraband, but I know they want to steal our stuff. I empty my pockets before they can do it for me, and I pull out a can of baked beans, my wallet, my phone, and a pocket watch. I give a guy my beans so he doesn't take anything else. I tell them I have a NyQuil in my wallet if they want it, but they don't.

We go to the warehouse and steal some clothes. On our way back, Alex is also there. First, we go up a bunch of stairs, but they have weird obstacles so you have to crawl up them through a little hole. Then we reach the colorful metal poles again, and we have to climb up, then slide down to get back to the dorms.

We start sliding down, but Alex picked the wrong ones to slide down, and after a while we fall into the abyss and die.

We respawn in a sort of airport like place, where some people are killing each other. It is likely hell. The guy in charge is upset because people aren't supposed to be able to die, but a guy got shot and died. Boss guy is talking on his phone to his boss, who tells him that people can die in hell now because they decided to get more evil.

End of dream.
