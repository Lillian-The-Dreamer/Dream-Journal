The Last Million

A bad dream

I was part of this team that had to do various illegal missions, and they weren't very pleasant.

The first one was we had to go into a gas station and kill the 2 employees there and steal all the money, but I got into a fistfight with one of them and i just couldn't put any power into my attacks at all, like I was underwater

And eventually we did it but then right before the cops got there, we realized we forgot to actually kill one of them and they were just unconscious, so we had to go real fast to do that and then kill a cop and then jump in a river to get away and hide

And then the second mission, we were in some enemy base and we were befriending them and had to help them move 11 million dollars cash from one of their vehicles to another

But then in the last hour of the mission we had to take the last 1 million dollars and put it in our vehicle, then jump into a different huge vehicle, drop down some chutes into mini tanks, blow up all the enemy gear, then escape into a third vehicle that had a drill so it could escape underground by drilling into a well and using that to connect out to the ocean

And I was very very stressed about this because it seemed very unlikely to work, so I was hanging out with one of the enemy girls (but like she wasn't military she just happened to be there and be with the enemy) and she was making music in the same music software that Phoenix uses, and I was hugging her and telling her that my mission sucked and I didn't want to do it, and she said that after I did it I should quit because it seemed like an awful life but I told her that the money was too good

And it did work out and we did the mission

And then we went back to train at our base camp (which was my house,) and we were target shooting, but like every time I shot, it ignited a big fire in the fields we were shooting at, which immediately spread to my woodshed and was burning all our wood and then we had to get big fire helicopters to dump water on stuff and try to salvage the wood

And then Phoenix was there and he said he wanted cotton candy because there was a guy selling cotton candy, but I said that I hated cotton candy so I didn't want any

End of dream
