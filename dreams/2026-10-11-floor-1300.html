Floor 1300

A good dream

I was in a class, but it had just ended. My friends and I were going to go clubbing, but I had my backpack (which was actually just a very large cloth sack) and the wrong outfit on, so I had to go back to my dorm first to drop my stuff off and change.

I go outside and it is smuggs (but the dream version that I often have, where there is a sterling 2 lift.) I'm at the top of the mountain, so I ski down to the lift, and when I get there I do a cool spin move.

At the bottom is the small house, which I go into to drop my stuff off by the fireplace. It's just someone's house, but everyone is congregating there like a ski lodge. I exit and go up the ski lift.

When I get to the top, I realize I forgot something, so I go back down. I enter the house, but I must have entered the wrong one, because it seems to just be some different house that's big and for rich people.

I find a woman inside, and ask her about it, and she is understanding because people keep coming into her house thinking it's the other one. I go to leave, but I have to sneak out really fast so the rest of the family doesn't see me and attack me for invading their home.

I exit on the top floor, and come out in some sort of office building. I roam around until I find a door that says Champlain college. I enter, and it seems to be an event for freshmen. I ask one of the students working there how I can get to the ski lodge again, and he says he has no clue. He asks if I want to be part of the event and I say no because I'm a Junior. He says okay.

There is a railing and then a really big drop on the other side of it, so I vault over it and grab the railing before I fall. The guy yells at me and asks why I would do that, but I don't answer.

I start sliding down a pole, and it turns into me playing slay the spire 1. I'm performing an exploit where when you play as the silent, you can slide down past everything and get to floor 1300, which is at the bottom.

The guy asks me how I'm gonna beat the floor 1300 boss with just my starting deck and no relics or anything.

I pick up 3 secret relics. Two of them seem unimportant, but the third lets me transform 3 cards in my deck.

I transform a strike into a card that says "apply 4 venomous, your attacks deal 4 less damage." This card seems really bad so I decline it. My second strike gets transformed into an attack that costs 0 and says "deal 4 damage for every skill played this turn." I take this one, and then go back to take the first one too, because I thought they would combo really well. (Even though I can't think of 2 cards that could combo worse)

My third transform was a focus strike, which I didn't take because I don't have orbs.

I start fighting the boss, which is the queen from sts2. She is very easy and I win.

End of dream
