Bad Animals

A bad dream

It was dusk and me and someone (maybe Lindy) we're on our deck, looking out into the woods. I noticed something in the woods down by the field to the left (princess mononoke style) and a very large moose came out of the woods. It was acting erratic and coming closer so we went back inside and locked the door. It came up on the deck and just stood perfectly still and stared into the house, which was very scary, because I knew that this was a very malicious and dangerous creature that wanted to kill us. Up close it was unlike any moose ever, it had extremely large antlers and fucked up incomplete stripes kind of like a zebra. It was also huge in the front half and shorter in the back, kind of like a gorilla 🦍. I decided to go to sleep since there was nothing else I could do, and in the morning the moose was gone.

I then decided to go on a walk. I walked for a while on a bunch of those little planks that go over mud, and after a while I was in a swamp type location and there were many garter snakes that were very large (like 2 feet or so) and also other creatures. I was sitting by a pond area and a snake came up to me and bit my arm, which was annoying but didn't hurt that bad. After a while it detached itself and went away, but it went into the water and died for some reason. A bird was also dead in the pond but I knew it was because it got bitten by a snake and the venom paralyzed it. After a little bit I got bitten by another snake, this time it slithered into my sleeve and bit me and held on for like 5 minutes, and it hurt really bad. I was on the ground in pain and Henry showed up so I asked him to help me with the snake and he said no. But it was okay because it detached and got out of my sleeve anyway. When it came out of my sleeve I realized it wasn't a snake but actually a gila monster, which is why is hurt so bad. Then my mom showed up and I told her that a bunch of animals bit me. I also said that it wasn't as bad as the time that it was sharks. I showed her a tier list of most painful/most common types of bites I received, and shark was at the top with 8 bites that were very painful. Also up there was some kind of fish that in my mind was similar to a coelacanth mixed with a sturgeon. And #3 was some kind of fictional thing that was like a little round guy with a fin and a big mouth.

My mom asked if I'd ever just been rammed into instead of bitten and I told her no, that would be ridiculous.

We then went home to prepare to go to a wedding. I left early for some reason without getting my outfit, which was a bad idea. During my drive I saw a sign for Los Angeles which was not where I wanted to go, but I slid on the snow anyway and accidentally took that exit. While driving, I got into an argument with some woman I was dating. She was mad and called me a finance conference digger, as in I was only with her so I could go with her to finance conferences. This was totally the case but I denied it. I called my mom to ask about the dress code for the wedding, and she said 1300s fashion, because the guests don't go online very much so they wouldn't know the new fashion.

After I hung up, I spun out of control on the highway and crashed upside down. We survived, and the woman in the car with me apologized for calling me a finance conference digger.

End of dream
