Centipedes

A bad dream

It was late and I was tired. I was sitting at my desk turning off my PC, when I saw a little tiny bug on my headphones. I didn't want it there obviously so I flicked it off. Then I got into bed, and looking up at the ceiling, there was an extremely large centipede, like 2 feet long and the girth of my arm. I started screaming for Lindy, and she got out of bed to look at it. She was remarkably unconcerned, but I eventually got her to agree to help get rid of it.

After searching for it for a while, since it had crawled away, she found it and got it to crawl onto a big stick. She was walking towards me with the stick and shoving it in my face, even though I was screaming for her to stop walking towards me. Eventually she got close enough that the centipede bit me, though it didn't hurt very bad.

She said that she didn't notice I was standing there, and I should've told her to not do that, and I got very mad because I was literally screaming at her to not do that.

She brought it downstairs, and I followed. When I got downstairs, it had become the size of a very small centipede. Lindy said that this species only grew in Maine, so it would obviously become small in Vermont. I thought that was bad logic.

She was taking it outside, and I wanted to throw up since I suspected that it had implanted eggs into my stomach when it bit me. I ran upstairs to switch my phone to the app that let me throw up. In this dream there was a mechanic that it took 16 seconds to change apps, and they were really long seconds so it took a while.

I got upstairs and when I walked into my room, there was another centipede, so I yelled for Lindy to come back. She said that this one didn't seem that threatening so we could just leave it be (it was identical to the first one)

I got my mom to get up, and she got Lindy to agree to put this one outside too. I went back on my PC to check if Henry was playing Counter Strike, but discord said that he'd been playing BBC News for 9 hours. Lindy said that it was because there was a wait-list for the news now, since they played the daily news for 1 person at a time, so you had to wait in a virtual queue for it.

I finally got my phone changed to the throwing up app.

End of dream
